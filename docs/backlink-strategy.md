# Backlink Strategy — AdyDaddy

Backlinks (links from other sites to yours) are one of the strongest ranking
signals for both classic search and AI answer engines. They **cannot be
generated from inside this repo** — they require other sites to link to you.
Most steps below need your accounts and a few minutes of manual setup; they
are listed roughly in order of ROI.

## Priority 1 — Profiles that are also links (do these first)

- **Google Business Profile** (business.google.com): create/claim "AdyDaddy",
  category "Marketing agency", add website, phone, and service areas. Link:
  https://www.adydaddy.com/
- **LinkedIn Company Page**: create the company page and link the website.
- **Instagram** (`adydaddyofficial`): put the site link in the bio.
- **Crunchbase**: create a free company profile with the website URL.
- **Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush**: create agency profiles. These are the
  directories buyers actually use for agencies, and they pass authority.

## Priority 2 — Real, citable content (attracts links organically)

- Publish the **case studies** (once real ones replace the samples) — real,
  specific numbers get cited and linked.
- Publish **original research / a benchmark** (e.g. "D2C ROAS benchmarks in
  India") — data posts earn links and AI citations.
- Write one or two **definitive guides** (e.g. "Payback period for D2C brands").

## Priority 3 — Outreach (manual)

- Guest posts / contributed articles on marketing and D2C/e-commerce blogs.
- Podcast appearances (D2C/e-commerce/marketing podcasts in India).
- Ask **clients** to link to you from their sites, and list yourself in the
  **partner directories** of Shopify, Meta, and Google.

## What NOT to do

- Do not buy links or use bulk directory / PBN submission services — Google
  penalizes these, and they will hurt more than help.
- Avoid "1000 backlinks in 24 hours" tools.

## Technical prerequisites (already in place)

- `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` (both reference the canonical www domain).
- `rel=canonical` on every page.
- JSON-LD: Organization, Person, WebSite, Service, Review, FAQ, Breadcrumb.
- `llms.txt` + `llms-full.txt` for AI/answer-engine crawlers.
